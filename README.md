## Project: Superstore Sales Analysis

### Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data from a global superstore to identify 
regional and category-level performance patterns, using Excel PivotTables for 
data aggregation and analysis.

### Key Findings

**1. Regional Performance**
- West ($710K) and East ($670K) regions generate over 60% of total sales
- South region underperforms significantly ($389K), the lowest of all four regions

**2. Category Performance**
- Technology leads overall sales ($827K), followed by Furniture ($729K) 
  and Office Supplies ($705K)
- The three categories are relatively balanced, suggesting a diversified 
  product mix rather than dependency on a single category

**3. Regional × Category Cross-Analysis**
- East region's Technology sales ($263K) is the single highest 
  region-category combination in the dataset — even exceeding West's 
  Technology sales, despite West having higher overall sales
- South underperforms consistently across all three categories, not just 
  in aggregate — indicating a region-wide weakness rather than a 
  category-specific issue
- West shows the most balanced distribution across categories 
  ($217K–247K range), unlike other regions where one category dominates

### Recommendations
- Investigate why South underperforms across all categories — potential 
  causes include lower store density, market saturation, or demographic 
  differences
- Explore what's driving East's strong Technology performance and consider 
  replicating successful strategies (marketing, inventory, partnerships) 
  in other regions
